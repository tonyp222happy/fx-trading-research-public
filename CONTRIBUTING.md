# Contributing

Contributions are welcome after the repository contribution and licensing policy is finalized.

Research changes should include:

- a clear hypothesis,
- deterministic strategy logic,
- the exact experiment configuration,
- data provenance or a redistributable sample,
- backtest and benchmark metrics,
- walk-forward evidence when parameters are tuned,
- a reproducibility report, and
- negative or rejected findings when relevant.

Do not submit broker credentials, live account information, proprietary datasets, copied third-party transcripts, or private production strategy code.
